# Annotation Guidelines

## Overview

This document describes the annotation rules used in this portfolio.

---

## Sentiment

### Positive
The customer expresses satisfaction, appreciation, or happiness.

Example:
"I really love this product."

---

### Negative

The customer reports a problem, dissatisfaction, or frustration.

Example:
"My order never arrived."

---

### Neutral

The customer asks for information or requests assistance without expressing strong emotion.

Example:
"Can you help me change my password?"

---

## Intent

### Statement

Provides information without expecting action.

### Question

Requests information.

### Request

Asks someone to perform an action.

### Complaint

Reports a problem or dissatisfaction.

---

## Category

- Account
- Payment
- Shipping
- Product
- Technical

---

## Reason

Every annotation includes a short explanation describing why the selected labels were chosen.
